Synthetic incident response playbook (demo content for RAG evaluation — not production policy)

Severity and paging (illustrative)
- Sev-1 / customer-impacting: page incident commander + on-call SRE within 5 minutes; exec comms template IC-EXEC-01.
- Sev-2: page owning team + optional secondary; update status page if external blast radius.
- Sev-3/4: business hours unless error budget burn exceeds 25% in 1h.

Escalation matrix
- High priority open incidents (examples from synthetic catalog: INC-1001, INC-1004, INC-1008, INC-1015, INC-1021, INC-1025, INC-1027, INC-1029): war-room optional; post customer-safe updates every 30 minutes until mitigated.
- Medium (INC-1003, INC-1007, INC-1010, INC-1012, INC-1017, INC-1019, INC-1023, INC-1024, INC-1031): owning team drives; escalate to platform if blast radius crosses two services or CHG linkage appears.
- Security / compliance (INC-1005, INC-1013, INC-1019, INC-1024, INC-1025): loop in security-team lead; no public post until SEC- review.
