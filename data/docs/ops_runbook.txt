Incident escalation policy:
1) Sev-1 incidents must be acknowledged within 5 minutes.
2) Escalate to on-call platform engineer after 10 minutes if unresolved.
3) Notify incident commander and security lead for customer-impacting events.

Backup retention policy:
- Production backups are retained for 35 days.
- Staging backups are retained for 14 days.

AWS key rotation policy:
- IAM access keys must be rotated every 90 days.
